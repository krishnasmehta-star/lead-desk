The whole job in one line

When a lead comes in: put it in the tracker, call within 2 hours, and always write down when you will follow up next. That is it. The ops manager owns every lead until it is Booked or Not going ahead.

3 · How fast, strictly

Reply times we do not miss

People send enquiries to two or three travel companies on the same evening. Whoever calls back first usually gets the booking.

2 hours

First call

Leads between 9am and 7pm, Mon to Sat. No answer? Send a WhatsApp right away.

By 10am

Late leads

Anything after 7pm or on Sunday gets called by 10am the next working day.

1 day

Our public promise

The website promises a person will reply. Going past one working day breaks that.

24 hours

Quote out

Once you have their trip details, the plan and price go out within 24 hours.

Day 2 · 5 · 10

Follow-ups

After a quote, follow up on these days. Note every call in the tracker.

4 tries

When to stop

Four real attempts over 7 days, no reply: close it as "No response".

Four rules, no exceptions

1. No lead ends the day outside the tracker. 2. Every open lead has a follow-up date. 3. Nothing is closed without a reason. 4. Nothing is deleted. Mistakes are closed as "Duplicate or test".

4 · The first call

5 minutes, 4 questions

ASK 1When do you want to go?
ASK 2How many people? Any kids or seniors?
ASK 3Roughly what budget?
ASK 4Is anyone else deciding with you?

End every call with a time: "I will send you a plan and price by 4pm tomorrow." Then do it.

5 · Every Monday

Read the numbers at the top of the Leads tab

New leads last week · how many were called within 2 hours (aim for 9 out of 10) · how many are late now · how many booked. Other targets get set after a month of real numbers.
